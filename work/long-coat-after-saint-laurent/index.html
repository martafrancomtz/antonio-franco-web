Long coat — study after Saint Laurent

2026Study afterWool melton

The brief

I wanted to work on length. A long coat is the garment where a small mistake in the shoulder shows all the way down to the hem.

The pattern

Adapted from a coat block. I took it to mid-calf and moved the balance towards the back, so the fronts hang straight instead of swinging open — which a teal wool melton of this weight does very easily.

What I learned

The hardest part was the hem: the melton was too thick to turn twice, so I faced it instead. I learned to finish thick cloth by taking bulk out, not by adding stitches.
