Bomber jacket — study after Zara

2024Study afterUpholstery cloth

The brief

The first time I copied a garment from the market. I chose a men’s bomber, took the shape apart, and redrew it until my pattern matched the original.

The pattern

Adapted from an existing block, then reworked. The cloth was a very thick upholstery fabric with a great deal of structure — nothing like a jacketing wool — and the pattern had to change to suit it.

I added ease across the back and lowered the sleeve head, because the cloth could not turn a tight curve, and cut the seam allowances away at the collar and cuffs so the ribbing joined flat. The upholstery cloth was a choice: I wanted a jacket that could almost stand up on its own.

What went wrong

Six weeks, because I made the sleeves twice. The first pair kept the sleeve head of the original, and in this cloth it would not ease into the armhole — it folded instead of curving. I lowered the cap, took the ease out and set them again.

What I learned

I learned more here than anywhere else. If I had to keep one thing from everything I have learned so far, it would be this jacket.

What I’d do differently

I would make a full toile in a cloth of the same weight before cutting the real fabric.
